TheTribunal

Four advocates and three judges deliberate one charge sheet in seven separate model calls.

The three opinions are presented side by side and are never combined.

A fictional proceeding. Each judge adapts a judicial method from a real jurist's published opinions; no judge represents the jurist or predicts how they would decide.
