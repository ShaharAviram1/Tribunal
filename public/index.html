The Tribunal

A fictional proceeding. Each judge adapts a judicial method from a real jurist's published opinions; no judge represents the jurist or predicts how they would decide.

Four advocates and three judges deliberate one charge sheet in seven separate model calls. The three judicial opinions are presented side by side and are never combined.
